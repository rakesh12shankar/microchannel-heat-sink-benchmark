# Inputs and source records

CAD files are original scripted SolidWorks reconstructions. Five verified variants are included; the rejected early depth exports are excluded. The nominal model is Qu_UnitCell; depth variants use their verified unique filenames. STEP lengths are millimeters; simulation lengths are meters.

The small digitization CSVs contain derived readings from Fig.7 of Qu and Mudawar (2002), DOI 10.1016/S0017-9310(01)00337-4. They are not author-provided raw data. See docs/digitization.md for calibration and assigned uncertainty. The paper and full-text extraction are referenced but excluded.
